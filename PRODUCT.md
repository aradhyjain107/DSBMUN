# Product Specification — DSB MUN 5.0

## 1. Vision & Purpose
DSB MUN 5.0 is the 5th annual Model United Nations conference hosted by DSB International Public School on **1–2 August 2026**. 
The web app serves as the single source of truth for delegates, faculty advisors, and parents to explore committee agendas, daily schedules, dress code guidelines, leadership details, and delegate perks.

## 2. Target Audience
- **Primary**: High school students (Grades 8–12, ages 14–18) in the New Delhi area and national MUN circuit.
- **Secondary**: Faculty advisors, school administrators, and parents.
- **Audience Expectation**: High visual polish, retro-tech / tactile UI aesthetics (macOS 10.0 window chrome, paper zine texture, starburst badges), instant mobile responsiveness, zero layout glitching.

## 3. Key Features & Page Sections
1. **Hero Viewport**: Asymmetric lockup with Bebas Neue typography, primary wordmark (`DSB` red, `MUN` lime, `5.0` navy), rotating fingerprint SVG, and real-time countdown timer.
2. **Director General & Leadership Messages**: Mac OS X window chrome containers with full-color leadership portraits, expandable letters, and interactive decline button easter egg.
3. **Committees Directory**: Asymmetric grid showcase of 9 committees with circular navy SVG seals, misregistration cyan ghost hover effects, and agenda summaries.
4. **Finder App Schedule & Dress Code**: Interactive macOS Finder UI with sidebar tabs for Day 1 & Day 2 schedules, dress code rules (Day 1: Indian Traditional, Day 2: Western Formal), and committee incharges.
5. **Perks & AirDrop Experience**: AirDrop-styled UI card with delegate perks, custom photobooth filters, and interactive Easter eggs.
6. **Footer & Attribution**: Event location, contact channels, and credits for Website Creator & Head of Social Media (Aradhy Jain).

## 4. Brand Tone & Aesthetics
- **Voice**: Empathetic, logical, confident, diplomatic.
- **Visual Style**: Warm paper substrate (`#F2EBDC`), solid navy accents (`#0E2A8A`), high-contrast display typography (`Bebas Neue`), serif accents (`Georgia` italic), and clean sans-serif body copy (`Inter`).
- **Anti-Patterns to Avoid**: Generic dark mode SaaS cards, floating blur glassmorphism, monochrome leadership filters, layout thrashing timers.
