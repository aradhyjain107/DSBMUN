About DSB MUN 5.0

DSB MUN 5.0 is the fifth annual Model United Nations conference organized and hosted by DSB International Public School, located in Rishikesh, Uttarakhand, India. Scheduled for 1–2 August 2026, the conference brings together over 400 student delegates, faculty advisors, and MUN veterans across 9 specialized committees.

Our Theme: Evolution for Revolution

The core theme of DSB MUN 5.0 — Evolution for Revolution — reflects the belief that global diplomacy requires constant intellectual evolution to solve modern crises. Delegates debate pressing international issues ranging from regional Middle East conflict resolution and human rights in UNHRC, to constitutional secularism in AIPPM and Lok Sabha, strategic arms control in UNGA, and ocean governance under UNCLOS.

Conference Structure & Committees

DSB MUN 5.0 features nine simulation committees tailored for high school students (Grades 6 through 12):

  • UNHRC (United Nations Human Rights Council) — Grades 6–12
  • UNSC (United Nations Security Council) — Grades 8–12
  • Lok Sabha (House of the People, Parliament of India) — Grades 8–12
  • AIPPM (All India Political Parties Meet) — Grades 8–12
  • UNGA (United Nations General Assembly) — Grades 8–12
  • UNCSW (UN Commission on the Status of Women) — Grades 8–12
  • IP (International Press) — Journalism, Photography & Caricature
  • IPL Auction (Indian Premier League Mega Auction) — Strategy & Bidding
  • UNCLOS (UN Convention on the Law of the Sea) — Maritime Security

Conference Leadership

The conference is overseen by Principal Mr. Shiv Sehgal, Secretary-General Ishika Dhamanda, Director-General Plaksha, and Website Creator & Head of Social Media Aradhy Jain.
