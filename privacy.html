Privacy Policy & Event Terms

Last Updated: 1 August 2026

At DSB MUN 5.0, hosted by DSB International Public School, we take student data privacy and security seriously. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information collected from delegates, advisors, and visitors, how it is processed, and our strict non-retention commitment.

1. Information We Collect

DSB MUN 5.0 collects only the information voluntarily submitted through official registration forms or contact inquiries. This includes:

  • Delegate Full Name, Grade, and Age
  • School / Institution Name
  • Contact Phone Number and Email Address
  • Committee Allotment Preferences and Portfolio Requests

2. Use of Information

All collected information is used strictly and exclusively for conference administration, committee allocation, delegate badge verification, and sending essential event updates (such as background guides or schedule changes).

3. Third-Party Sharing & Non-Retention Policy

Zero Third-Party Sharing: Delegate data is never sold, shared, rented, or disclosed to any third-party marketing agencies, advertisers, or external organizations.

Data Non-Retention: Personal registration data collected for DSB MUN 5.0 is retained solely for the duration of the conference logistics and will not be retained after the conclusion of the event on 2 August 2026.

4. AI Agent Access & Content Negotiation

This web application supports automated AI crawlers, search indexing engines, and Model Context Protocol (MCP) agents. Non-personal conference data (such as committee agendas, schedule, dress code, and venue details) is publicly queryable via /llms.txt, /sitemap.xml, and /.well-known/mcp.json.

5. Contact Us

For questions or privacy requests, please contact the DSB MUN 5.0 Secretariat at dsbmun@gmail.com.
